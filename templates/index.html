    🗺️ HOW TO GET THERE

    🧭 Google Maps 🍎 Apple Maps 🚙 Waze

    🚗 Driving & Parking

    Stadium lots require a pre-purchased parking pass. Downtown garages near the Inner Harbor are a 10–20 min walk. Leave early — I-95 and Russell St get spicy.

    🚊 Light Rail

    MTA Light RailLink stops at Camden Yards and Hamburg Street (right next to the stadium). The easiest way to skip the parking chaos.

    🚆 MARC Train

    The Camden Line drops you at Camden Station, a short walk north of the stadium. Check MARC's weekend schedule — service varies.

    🚶 Walk-up Snacks

    Federal Hill and the Inner Harbor are both walkable for pre- and post-game food. Pickles Pub on Washington Blvd is a classic pregame spot.

    🎒 STADIUM SURVIVAL GUIDE

    👜 Clear Bag Policy

    NFL standard: clear bags up to 12″×6″×12″, or a one-gallon clear plastic bag, plus one small clutch about the size of a hand (≈4.5″×6.5″). Diaper bags/medical bags get inspected.

    💳 Cashless

    M&T Bank Stadium is cashless. Bring cards / phone pay. Cash-to-card kiosks exist if you're vintage.

    📱 Tickets

    Mobile tickets only — in the Ravens app / Ticketmaster. Add to Wallet the night before. Screenshots don't scan.

    👂 For the Kids

    It gets LOUD. Earplugs or kids' ear protection are a great idea, especially for 3rd-down defense.
